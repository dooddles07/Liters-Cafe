# Image credits

One real photo, twelve stock placeholders.

`storefront-glass.jpg` is the actual San Felipe branch, converted from the WebP
you left in `/images` at the project root. Everything else is Unsplash stock and
is not Liters.

The stock files are standing in until real photography exists. Replacing one is a
drag-and-drop: save your photo over the same filename and the site picks it up
with no code change.

Photographer names aren't listed below. Unsplash blocks unauthenticated access to
its photo pages, so they couldn't be retrieved without an API key. The photo ID
is recorded instead, which is enough to find the original. Nothing here is
invented.

## Rejected during review

Three candidates were pulled and thrown out after actually looking at them.

The first was a cafe exterior with a legible "N°15 COFFEE SHOP" chalkboard and
"Le Petit Palace" signage, which would have put another business's branding on
the Liters site. The second was a daytime shot of a lighting store, picked for a
slot that needed a night storefront. The third was a bar with liquor shelving and
a neon sign, headed for the hero background of a family cafe.

If you swap any of these yourself, check the same three things. No other
business's name in frame, no readable foreign signage, and the dish actually
matches its label.
